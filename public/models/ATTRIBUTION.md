# Geometry and scientific sources

**Original anatomical geometry:** Human Reference Atlas (HRA), HuBMAP, united male and female reference object sets v1.5. Medical illustration by Kristen Browne, Heidi Schlehlein and contributing HRA teams. Based on the National Library of Medicine Visible Human data; the included Allen brain structures retain Allen Institute provenance.

Changes: selected astronaut-health-relevant structures; combined constituent meshes into selectable organ groups; reduced polygon counts; retained source spatial registration; translated the model origin to floor level; applied a navy presentation palette. No new organs, simulated measurements, or patient-specific anatomy were generated. Placenta, umbilical structures, and the separately modeled Yao lymph node were excluded. - CSA radiation monitoring: https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/news/articles/2026/2026-02-26-monitoring-radiation-astronaut-health.asp

NASA's sources guide which systems are relevant, not the mesh geometry. Radiation exposure requires dosimetry; an organ visualization or normal vital signs cannot measure radiation dose. Body temperature is not a test of immune function. Sleep/mood are not brain scans; glucose is not a pancreatic or liver function test. Eye, bone, muscle, kidney and immune assessments need additional measures beyond the eight v1 indicators.

This is an atlas-derived educational interface, not a complete anatomy atlas, diagnostic device, surgical model or clinical validation. The bone and muscle layers contain only structures supplied and selected from these atlas sets. Anatomical variation exists between people and the two reference sets. Simplification may remove small surface detail. Blue colors, transparency and highlighting are illustrative, not tissue appearance or measured health status.
